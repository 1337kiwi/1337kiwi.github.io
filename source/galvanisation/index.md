# What is this? 

This sticker can serve as an admission ticket to one of the secret sessions at CackalackyCon 2026.  There will be two separate instances of said workshop...

# Friday night 7:30 PM - 9:00 PM
## Suite _
# Saturday night 7:30 PM - 9:00 PM
## Dogwood

This sticker allows one seat of attendance to one of these secret session ONLY if it is signed by Kiwi or Deviant. 

If it is signed in BLUE ink, that is admission to the Friday night workshop.  

If it is signed in RED ink, that is admission to the Saturday night workshop.  

If your sticker is not signed, it is not a ticket... but it is nonetheless a very nice sticker.

Also please be advised: after 7:30 PM, much like Cinderella's carriage, this ticket no longer means anything magical and it reverts back to being simply a sticker. 

Again: this ticket doesn't mean a damn thing and has zero value if you fail to show up on time.  Thank you and enjoy!
