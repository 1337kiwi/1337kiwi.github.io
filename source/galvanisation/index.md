# What is this secret session? 

This is a hands-on workshop exploring historical and modern devices used for electrical stimulation of nerves and muscles. We'll cover device history, how electrical stimulation affects the body, and safe demonstration practices. Devices both mild and more powerful will be shown; attendees may have the opportunity to experience some devices under strict supervision.

# Introduction

Many people in the world are content to live life on their default OS, seldom challenging themselves with new experiences, and often hesitant to try something out of the ordinary. There is virtue in curiosity, strength in exploration, and potential in energy. 

As hackers, we are seldom so constrained by tradition and fear. We push past our own limits, challenge our preconceived notions, and leap at the chance to experiment with new ways of thinking and feeling. 

In this quest for sensation, we subject our bodies to a wide range of stimuli. We ride roller coaster or jump out of airplanes. We soak in hot springs or plunge into icy lakes. And some of us... well, we subject our corporeal form to electric impulses in order to see how our bodies respond. If you've ever touched a 9 volt battery to the tip of your tongue, you've already taken the first tentative steps into the world of electrical stimulation!

In this workshop, we will share the fascinating history of various devices that have been engineered since almost the very start of humanity's understanding of electricity with the aim of applying this fascinating energy to our nerves and muscles. Devices both old and new, both mild and powerful, will be showcased and available for you to experience firsthand. We will walk through the body’s experience of electricity, and learn how organs react to and handle it. 

Plenty of safety discussion will be involved and absolutely no one will be subject to any electric stimuli of any kind without their explicit consent.

If you've ever been curious about devices like Nikola Tesla’s Violet Ray Machine (aka Violet Wand) or wished you could generate sparks out of your own fingertips that would tingle those around you, this is your opportunity to make a foray into this world!

*NOTE: While it is true that electric stimuli have at times been associated with communities and events that have a decidedly adult theme, this specific workshop is deliberately all-ages appropriate. No materials explicitly related to adult themes will be the focus of the event and no slides or associated content will be NSFW. However, for safety and liability reasons, no participants under the age of 18 will be permitted to be the direct subjects of any electrical stimuli, with or without parental consent.*

**If you have a pacemaker, implanted medical device, epilepsy, pregnancy, or other relevant medical condition, please do not participate in direct stimulation -- speak with staff for alternatives.**
